Creamy Garlic Pasta

A simple and delicious creamy garlic pasta that is easy to prepare and perfect for a quick meal.

🥗 Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 3 cloves, garlic
  • 1 cup fresh cream
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt to taste
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

👩‍🍳 Preparation Steps

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water according to the package instructions.
  2. Heat butter in a pan and saute the garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add fresh cream and stir gently for a few minutes.
  4. Add Parmesan cheese, salt, and black pepper.
  5. Add the cooked pasta and mix everything together.
  6. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve hot.

Enjoy your homemade pasta! 🍝
